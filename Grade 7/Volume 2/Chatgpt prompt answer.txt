Create a modern futuristic neon arcade game called "Neon Drift" using React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and shadcn/ui.
The game should feel like a premium browser game with smooth animations, glowing neon visuals, reactive particles, and highly polished UI/UX.


Core Gameplay: The player controls a glowing geometric cube/rect. The cube automatically moves forward continuously from left to right. On every click/tap, the movement direction switches diagonally:
First click → move diagonally upward at 45 degrees Second click → move diagonally downward at -45 degrees Repeated clicking alternates between the two directions.
The objective is to avoid obstacles and survive as long as possible. Difficulty gradually increases over time.
Smooth collision detection and responsive controls are important.
